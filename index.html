Good to Know

Pre-Booking FAQs

Most-asked questions first — tap any question to expand.

Most AskedDo I need to pay an advance to pre-book?

No. Pre-booking with Placewell Retail is free — you're simply registering your interest so we can prioritise your unit and call you the moment stock arrives.

Most AskedWhen will I actually get my phone?

Apple's India launch and sales date is 18 September 2026. We cannot guarantee your exact model, colour and storage combination will be in stock on day one — Apple allocates stock to retailers at its own discretion. Your unit will be dispatched to you as and when that exact configuration is supplied to our store, which may be on launch day or a little after. We'll keep you updated by WhatsApp or call.

Most AskedHow does the ₹1,000 loyalty discount work?

If you've purchased from Placewell before (any store, any product), tick the loyalty box in the form. Our team verifies your past purchase against our records and applies ₹1,000 off at billing — it isn't auto-applied on this page since we check it manually.

Most AskedHow does the cashback offer work?

Apple's official India store currently lists up to ₹15,000 instant cashback with American Express, Axis Bank or ICICI Bank cards (minimum transaction ₹10,001). The exact amount depends on your specific card. Tell us your bank in the form and we'll confirm the precise cashback for your card before billing — this page never asks for your card number or bank login.

Most AskedCan I change my colour or storage choice later?

Yes — these are preferences to help us plan stock. Our team will reconfirm the exact configuration with you on WhatsApp or by call before billing.

Do you offer EMI and exchange on iPhone 18 Pro?

Yes. No-Cost EMI is available for 3 & 6 month tenures with most leading bank cards (subject to eligibility). For exchange, bring your old phone to any store — we evaluate it on the spot and offer the maximum trade-in value the market supports, adjusted directly against your new iPhone.

Can I cancel my pre-booking?

Yes, there's no obligation. Since no payment is collected at this stage, you can cancel anytime by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Does it support dual SIM in India?

Yes — iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max support dual SIM as nano-SIM + eSIM, with dual eSIM also available (eSIM activation depends on your carrier). Per apple.com/in specifications.

What's in the box, and what's the warranty?

The box includes the iPhone (running iOS 27) and a USB-C Charge Cable (1m) — the power adapter is sold separately, as with recent iPhones. Every unit carries Apple's standard 1-year limited warranty in India; ask our team about extended AppleCare options.
